## Overview

### Problem

Malaysian event-management SMEs face a significant challenge in coordinating customer requests, crews, equipment, and budgets, especially as demand fluctuates around festive periods. Relying on fragmented spreadsheets and messaging apps makes it difficult to detect staffing clashes, equipment shortages, and unrecorded expenses. This matters because these operational inefficiencies lead to inaccurate profit assumptions and unnecessary outsourcing, while also causing workload and scheduling conflicts for the workforce. Solving this problem is critical so that customers receive timely answers, workers get clear reporting times, and managers obtain a single, reliable operational overview.

### Solution

OpusEvents employs a two-stage numerical AI pipeline that first predicts the exact crew and equipment needed for an event, and then uses those predictions to estimate the total costs and optimal selling price. Additionally, an OpenAI-powered planning assistant streams tailored managerial advice based on the SME's business facts. The system is highly valuable because it integrates these AI tools into a single, unified workflow connecting customer requests, managerial approvals, and worker assignments. Economically, this allows SMEs to rapidly produce feasible quotations without relying on misleading profit assumptions or unnecessary outsourcing. Operationally, it drastically reduces manual planning effort while automatically preventing conflicting worker commitments and equipment shortages.

### Key Features

- **AI Resource and Cost Prediction Engine** —  A two-stage AI pipeline that first predicts the required crew and equipment for a requested event, and then uses those resource predictions to estimate the total costs and optimal selling price.
- **OpenAI-Powered Planning Assistant** — An integrated AI assistant that streams tailored managerial advice directly based on server-provided business facts.
- **Unified Role-Based Workflow and Calendar Integration** — A centralized system where customers submit booking requests, managers approve finances and adjust crew assignments, and workers view their assigned jobs on an exportable monthly calendar that prevents double booking.
